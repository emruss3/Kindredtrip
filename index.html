Why this matters for families

The four decisions that actually move the needle.

Most travel sites optimize for one decision at a time. Families are juggling four — and getting any of them wrong turns the trip. Here's how KindredTrips handles each one, with real examples.

①

Kids club minimum age

A 3-year-old at a "kids club 4+" resort means a babysitter.

Every resort says "kids club" but the floor age varies wildly. Iberostar Selection Bávaro accepts age 4; Hyatt Ziva Cap Cana accepts age 3; some 5★ properties don't take under 6. We pull the exact minimum age and surface it on the card.

See resorts with kids clubs that fit your kids' ages →
②

Family-of-5 room occupancy

"Sleeps 4" + a kid on the floor = two rooms or a different resort.

Most Caribbean resorts cap a standard room at 4 occupants. A family of 5 needs a suite, a connecting room, or a villa — usually 40-70% more. We pre-filter resorts to those that have the room type your family actually fits in, and tell you which configuration matches.

See resorts with connecting rooms or family suites →
③

Direct flight vs. connection

A connection with a 2-year-old can cost more than $300 in stress.

Booking shows hotels first; flights are an afterthought. We score flight ease as part of the rank — direct flights surface near the top automatically for families with little kids. A direct BNA → PUJ beats a connection BNA → AUA → BGI even when the second one looks cheaper on paper.

See only direct flights from your airport →
④

Total trip cost, including flights

A $250/night Aruba resort is not cheaper than a $320/night Punta Cana one.

$1,400 in connecting flights flips the math. We always show flight + hotel together for your exact party size and dates — never a misleading per-night nightly rate. That single change is the reason "the cheapest hotel" and "the cheapest trip" rarely agree.

Common questions

Frequently asked.

How is KindredTrips different from Booking.com or Expedia?

Booking and Expedia make you pick a Caribbean destination first, then optimize hotels and flights within that destination. KindredTrips works the opposite way: tell us your constraints (origin, dates, kids' ages, budget, must-haves) and we compare every flight + hotel combination across all 35 Caribbean countries and 79 airports — ranked by total trip cost. We also have family-specific filters those sites don't have, like kids-club minimum age.

Do you actually book the trip?

No. KindredTrips finds and ranks trips, but the actual booking happens on a trusted partner site like Booking.com or airline sites. The price you see and the price you pay are identical — we don't add fees. We earn a small affiliate commission when you book through our recommendations, which is how the site is free for users.

What Caribbean countries does KindredTrips cover?

All 35 Caribbean and Caribbean-adjacent countries, including Mexico, Dominican Republic, Bahamas, Jamaica, Cuba, Aruba, Turks and Caicos, Barbados, Saint Lucia, Antigua and Barbuda, Curacao, Puerto Rico, US Virgin Islands, Grenada, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, Saint Kitts and Nevis, Dominica, Trinidad and Tobago, Costa Rica, Belize, Honduras, Guatemala, Nicaragua, Panama, and Brazil's northeast coast.

Can I filter Caribbean resorts for young kids or infants?

Yes. KindredTrips offers filters for kids-club minimum age (some resorts accept children as young as 3), accepts-infants under 12 months, connecting rooms guaranteed, family room maximum occupancy, on-the-beach, lazy river, waterpark for little kids, and more. These are the details that determine whether a resort works for a family with young children.

Are KindredTrips prices live or estimates?

Prices on KindredTrips search results are estimated based on current market rates for your dates and family composition. When users click through to book on a partner site, that site shows live, bookable rates. Most of the time the live rate is within a few percent of our estimate. Estimated prices are clearly labeled.
