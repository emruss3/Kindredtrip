About KindredTrips.

Caribbean family travel, ranked by what fits your family. We compare across destinations — so you don't have to pick one before you see prices.

What we do

You tell us your origin airport, dates, and who's coming. We search 974 family resorts across 35 Caribbean countries and 79 destination airports — for your exact travelers and dates — and rank the results by total flight + hotel cost.

Family-fit details are surfaced, not buried: kids-club minimum age, on-the-beach, accepts-infants, connecting rooms guaranteed, water park for little kids, lazy river. The things that decide whether a resort actually works for your family.

What's different

Every other vacation tool makes you pick the destination first, then optimize within it. We do the opposite: tell us your constraints, we'll tell you which Caribbean destination, resort, and flight combo wins.

The difference shows up in real numbers. A direct flight to Punta Cana plus a 4-star resort often beats a connection to Aruba plus a "cheaper" property — once you add the flights, the math flips. We do that comparison across every Caribbean country at once, so the trip your family takes is actually the best one for your constraints.

How it works

  • You tell us who's coming. Origin, dates, adults, kids' ages. Toggle on must-haves like a kids club or direct-only flights.
  • We search every resort. 974 properties get priced for your exact family in seconds — no destination commitment.
  • You filter and book. Narrow by amenities your kids will actually use. Click through to book on a trusted partner. We route, we don't sell.

What we are NOT

  • Not a discount or coupon site.
  • Not an OTA pretending to be a curator.
  • Not a travel agency — we don't book your trip; we route you to a partner who does.
  • Not lifestyle content. No "10 things to pack!" listicles.
  • Not pretending to know everything. We say "estimated" when prices are estimated.

Where we are

This is an early version. The resort dataset is loaded; family-fit details are being filled in resort by resort. If a result looks wrong or a detail is missing, please tell us — that's how we get better.

What's next

  • Live rates from direct partnerships with Booking.com, Expedia, and Priceline.
  • Real resort photos — not stock — for every property.
  • Saved trips and price alerts for the search you're watching.
Why we're free

KindredTrips earns a referral fee when you book through our partners (Booking.com, airlines, etc.). The price you pay is identical — we don't add fees. We don't take payments directly; you book on the partner's site, they handle your reservation.

About our rankings

We sort by what fits your family — a mix of total trip cost, family-fit factors, and resort quality. We don't sort by which partner pays us the most. You can change the sort order any time using the dropdown above results.
