About KindredTrips.

A simple tool for one specific job: planning a Caribbean family vacation without 14 browser tabs.

What we do

KindredTrips searches every family-friendly resort in the Caribbean and every Caribbean airport at the same time, then ranks the complete trip — flights plus hotel — by total cost for your family. We surface only the resorts that fit kids, infants, and connecting rooms, with the family-fit details (kids-club ages, on-the-beach, accepts-infants) front and center.

Why we built it

Every other booking tool optimizes for one piece at a time. Booking.com is great for hotels but assumes you've already picked a destination. Skyscanner is great for flights but doesn't know what you're staying at. The big OTAs let you bundle — but only one destination at a time.

For a family trip, the choice that matters is total cost across destinations. A direct flight to Punta Cana might beat a connection to Aruba even if the resort is more expensive, because the flights are cheaper. KindredTrips does that math for you.

How it works

  • You tell us where you're flying from, when you want to go, and who's coming.
  • We search 974 family resorts across 35 Caribbean countries and price the full trip for your exact family size.
  • You see the top options ranked by total cost, with family-fit details front and center.
  • Click "See hotel rates" to book on a partner site. We earn a commission on confirmed bookings, at no extra cost to you.

Where we are

This is an early version. The resort dataset is loaded; the family-fit details (kids-club ages, on-beach status, etc.) are being added one resort at a time. If a result looks wrong or a detail is missing, please tell us — that's how we get better.

What's next

  • Real-time pricing through direct partnerships with Booking.com, Expedia, and Priceline.
  • Saved trips and price alerts.
  • Family-fit details on every resort.
