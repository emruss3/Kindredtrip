Beachfront family resorts in Belize — and how to reach each one

Every family arrives on an international flight into BZE. What happens next depends on where the resort is: the cayes and atolls need a boat or a short domestic flight, while the mainland coast is a road transfer. Leg times are shown only where verified.

Alaia Belize, Autograph Collection

Beach: Oceanfront. The resort notes a seawall, so direct water entry is limited; the Belize Barrier Reef Reserve is about 600 yards offshore. All-inclusive rates have been seen for this resort.

  1. International flight: Fly into BZE (Belize City).
  2. Onward to Ambergris Caye: Ambergris Caye is an island: take a short domestic flight to San Pedro or a water taxi from Belize City. Transfer time not verified — confirm with the resort.

Belizean Dreams Resort

Beach: Beachfront in Hopkins. Alcohol is not permitted at this property. Connecting/adjoining rooms exist on request.

  1. International flight: Fly into BZE (Belize City).
  2. Road transfer: About 1 h 52 min by road from BZE to Hopkins (routing estimate — confirm with the resort).

Thatch Caye, A Muy'Ono Resort

Beach: Private island with oceanfront cabanas and overwater bungalows. All-inclusive rates have been seen for this resort.

  1. International flight: Fly into BZE (Belize City).
  2. Onward to Dangriga: Continue south to the Dangriga area by domestic flight or road.
  3. Boat: Boat transfer out to the caye. Leg times not verified — confirm with the resort.

Manta Island Resort

Beach: Check directly with the resort.

  1. International flight: Fly into BZE (Belize City).
  2. Onward to Dangriga: Continue to the Dangriga area (domestic flight to DGA or road).
  3. Boat: Boat transfer out to Glover's Reef Atoll. Check directly with the resort for schedules.

Belize family resort FAQ

Which resorts in Belize allow children?

4 resorts in our Belize catalogue admit children: Alaia Belize, Autograph Collection, Belizean Dreams Resort, Thatch Caye, A Muy'Ono Resort, Manta Island Resort. Adults-only properties are excluded from this guide.

Which Belize resorts fit a family of four or five in one room?

Alaia Belize, Autograph Collection (up to 7 guests in its largest unit) and Belizean Dreams Resort (up to 6 guests in its largest unit). Thatch Caye, A Muy'Ono Resort's rooms sleep 2.

How do you get to Belize's beach resorts with kids?

Families fly into BZE (Philip S. W. Goldson International Airport (Belize City)). Resorts on the cayes and atolls then need a boat or a short domestic flight; Hopkins on the mainland is a road transfer. Each resort's full route is listed above.
