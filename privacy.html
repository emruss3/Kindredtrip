Privacy.

Plain-English summary of what we collect, why, and what we don't do.

What we collect

  • Search inputs — origin airport, dates, family size, and filters you set. We store these to run the search and to improve the product over time.
  • Outbound clicks — when you click "See hotel rates," we log which trip you clicked and the price displayed at the time. This is how booking partners attribute commission to us.
  • A session ID — a random identifier stored in your browser's local storage to link your clicks back to your search. It is not tied to your name or email.

What we don't collect

  • We don't ask for your name, email, or address. There are no accounts.
  • We don't sell your search data to third parties.
  • We don't run advertising trackers (no Google Ads, Facebook Pixel, etc.).

Cookies

We use a single piece of local storage (the session ID above) to associate your search with your booking clicks. We do not use third-party cookies on this site. The booking partners we redirect you to (Booking.com, Expedia, Priceline) have their own privacy policies and cookies — you'll be subject to those once you click through.

How affiliate partnerships work

When you click through to a booking partner, we pass an affiliate identifier so the partner can credit any resulting booking to KindredTrips. This is how we make money. You don't pay anything extra; the partner pays us a small percentage of their booking fee.

Data we may share with partners

The booking partner receives the destination and dates you searched for as part of the redirect URL. They do not receive any other personal information from us.

How long we keep data

Search inputs and click logs are kept indefinitely so we can improve the product and analyze trends. They are stored on Supabase (US-East). They are not exported or shared.

Your rights

If you'd like us to delete your search history, email us with your session ID (visible in your browser's local storage under kt_session_id) and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product evolves. The "last updated" date below will reflect any changes.
